About Me

Design is how I
tell your story.

My name is Jacob Mwangi, a creative graphic designer based in Nairobi. I combine a strong artistic eye with technical skills to produce designs that don't just look great — they work hard for your brand.

Certified in Graphic Design from NairoBits, I've worked across branding, print, digital, and social media — delivering clean, purposeful visuals for businesses, events, and individuals across Kenya and beyond.

My background in ICT gives me an edge in understanding digital platforms and how design functions in real-world environments.
